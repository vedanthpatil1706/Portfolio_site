🎨 Artify AI — AI-Powered Marketplace Assistant for Local Artisans

Empowering Traditional Craftsmanship through Generative AI and Data Intelligence.

🧩 Problem Statement

Local artisans often struggle to market their crafts, communicate their cultural stories, and reach wider digital audiences. Limited access to marketing resources and market insights makes sustainable business growth challenging.

💡 Proposed Solution

Artify AI is an AI-powered marketplace assistant designed to help local artisans showcase their creativity, generate compelling product stories, understand market trends, and strengthen their digital presence.

🌟 Key Features

  • ✍️ AI Story Generator: Generates authentic product stories and digital provenance cards using Gemini AI.
  • 📈 Trend Finder: Identifies trending keywords, hashtags, SEO opportunities, and market trends.
  • 🌱 Craft Score: Evaluates sustainability, material costs, energy usage, and pricing recommendations.
  • 📊 Market Demand Analysis: Provides market insights, product demand predictions, and growth opportunities.
  • 📢 Ad Creatives Generator: Creates promotional content and marketing ideas for social media and e-commerce platforms.
  • 📚 My Library: Stores generated stories, creative content, and AI-powered insights.

🧠 Technology Stack

Next.js React Tailwind CSS Firebase Gemini Flash 2.5 Cohere Command R+ Chart.js D3.js Git & GitHub

🌍 Impact & Innovation

Combines Generative AI, data analytics, and sustainability intelligence to support traditional artisans, preserve cultural craftsmanship, encourage eco-friendly practices, and create digital market opportunities.

🚌 Public Transport Delay Prediction Using Weather & Events

Analysing transport delays through weather, traffic, events, and scheduling data.

🎯 Project Overview

Developed a data science and machine learning project to explore public transport arrival delays using weather conditions, traffic congestion, event information, transport types, and scheduling data. The objective was to identify patterns and build regression models to predict arrival delays.

📊 Dataset

  • 📌 Total Records: 2,000
  • 📌 Original Features: 24
  • 🎯 Target Variable: actual_arrival_delay_min
  • 📂 Source: Kaggle public transport delay dataset

🔗 View Dataset on Kaggle

⚙️ Project Workflow

  • 1️⃣ Data loading and understanding
  • 2️⃣ Data cleaning and preprocessing
  • 3️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
  • 4️⃣ Feature engineering
  • 5️⃣ Train-test splitting
  • 6️⃣ Regression model development
  • 7️⃣ Model evaluation and comparison

🤖 Machine Learning Models

Linear Regression Random Forest XGBoost Gradient Boosting Mean Baseline

📈 Model Evaluation (MAE)

Mean Baseline: 8.212 Random Forest: 8.237 XGBoost: 8.263 Gradient Boosting: 8.314 Linear Regression: 8.418

🔍 Key Findings

  • Numerical correlations with the target were generally close to zero.
  • The available features showed weak predictive relationships with arrival delays.
  • None of the trained machine learning models outperformed the mean baseline.
  • The results highlighted the importance of data quality and feature relevance.

💡 Key Learning: Model evaluation is not just about building algorithms. Comparing results against a baseline helps identify whether machine learning actually adds predictive value.

🧰 Technology Stack

Python Pandas NumPy Matplotlib Scikit-learn XGBoost Jupyter Notebook

🚀 Future Improvements

Explore larger real-world datasets, incorporate historical delay information, route distance, vehicle details, and real-time traffic and weather data to improve model performance.
